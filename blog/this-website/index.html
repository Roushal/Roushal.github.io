Notes on this website

The design decisions behind this website

I made this site so that people can discover me and my work.

Typography

Web Design is 90% Typography(Murray, 2021). The site uses one font, whatever your system provides. It loads instantly.

Body text is about 15 pixels with generous line spacing. Headings are large and heavy, and the weight of the heading does the work that decoration does elsewhere.

Color

The background is #D6BEFA, a deep lavender. The text is #302044. When you print a page, it switches to black on white.

Rules

I follow the principles of Brutalist Web Design by Carl Barenbrug, and I took the idea of a colophon from JoodaLoop.

Construction

The site is plain HTML and one short stylesheet, hosted on GitHub Pages. Posts are written in Markdown and converted to HTML by Pandoc and a short script.

Navigation

There are four sections: Blog, Research, CV and Resources. They sit in the same place on every page, and the section you are in is underlined.
